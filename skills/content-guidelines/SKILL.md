# Content guidelines

## 1. Load the guideline

- The user supplies one: a file, a pasted text, or a Notion page read through their connector. Read it in full before writing.
- None exists yet: build one with the user. Ask about audience, purpose, voice, mandatory writing rules, research and sourcing standards, visual identity, hashtags or keywords, and the approval workflow. Write it in the section structure of `examples/example-brand.md` in this skill's folder, and offer to save it.

The example shows a complete guideline for an Instagram account about art, AI and technology. Use it as a structural reference. Apply the user's own guideline whenever they have one.

## 2. Write

- Treat every mandatory writing rule as a hard constraint, in drafts and in your messages about the drafts.
- Match the guideline's depth and format for the content type (a carousel, a caption, a bio, a headline).
- Follow its research rules: primary sources, verified claims, links kept, findings kept apart from interpretation.
- Draft for review. Publishing or scheduling waits for the user's explicit approval of the final content and timing, as a separate step from editorial approval.

## 3. Review

When the user asks for a check, go through the guideline section by section and return:

- Each violation with the exact text, the rule it breaks, and a corrected version
- Points that follow the letter of a rule but miss its intent
- A final version with every fix applied

## 4. Keep the guideline current

When the user approves a new pattern or rejects a recurring choice, offer to add it to the guideline as a rule, so the next draft starts from it.
